This file contains competency questions for the Federalist Papers, based on essays, letters, and claims. The competency questions are listed below:

## Module 1: Essays and authorship claims

1. Which essays are credited to person X, and by which sources?
2. What newspaper published essay X, and on what date?
3. Which essays have disputed authorship, and who claimed each one?
4. Which essays have joint authorship, and who are the authors of each?
5. Which source supports each authorship claim, and when was the claim made?
6. Who wrote Federalist Paper no. N, according to source S?
7. Which essays does every source credit to the same person?
8. How many essays does each founder get credit for, under each source?
9. Who made a given authorship claim, when, and in what kind of document?

## Module 2: Letter network

10. Who wrote to person X about the Federalist on date Y?
11. Which letters mention the Federalist or "Publius", and who sent each one to whom?
12. Which letters about the Federalist did person X send or receive?
13. Which letter is a reply to which?
14. Where was each letter written, and where was it sent?
15. Which letters were written while the essays were still being published (October 1787 to May 1788)?
16. Who else is named in a letter, besides the sender and the recipient?
17. Where does our text of each letter come from?

## Module 3: What the letters say

18. Which letters name the authors, and to whom were they sent?
19. Who sent copies of which volume, how many, to whom, through whom, and when?
20. Which essays does a given letter refer to?
21. Who knew who "Publius" was, and from what date?
22. Which authorship claims come from letters rather than from lists or editions?
23. Which letters give an opinion of the Federalist, and what passage says so?
24. Which letters mention a problem with the printed book?
