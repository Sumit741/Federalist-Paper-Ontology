This file contains competency questions for the Federalist Papers, based on essays, letters, and claims. The competency questions are listed below:

1. Which essays did X write?
2. Who wrote to X Franklin in date Y?
3. What newspaper published essay X, and on what date?
4. Which essays have disputed authorship, and who claimed each one?
5. Which essays have joint authorship, and who are the authors of each?
6. Which source supports each authorship claim, and when was the claim made?
7. Which documents are classified as Anti-Federalist essays?
8. Who are the co-authors of essays that have more than one author?
9. Who wrote Federalist Paper no. N, according to the source S.
10. Which essay does every source credit to the same person?
11. How many essays does each founder get credit for, under each source?
12. Who made the claim, when, and in what kind of document?
13. Which letter mentioned the Federalist and who sent them to whom?
14. Which letter reveals who the author was and to whom?
15. How were copies of the book sent around?
16. Which essays does a given letter refer to?
17. Which letters coordinate the distribution of the bound volumes to specific state ratifying conventions?
