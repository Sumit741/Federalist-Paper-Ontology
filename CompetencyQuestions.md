Competency Question List:

1. Which essays are credited to person P, and in which sources?  
2. Whom does source S credit as the author of essay E?  
3. Which essays are disputed, and whom does each source credit?  
4. Which essays do all sources credit to the same person(s)?  
5. Which essays have a joint claim, and whom does it credit?  
6. How many essays does each source credit to each person?  
7. For a given claim, what source records it, what type of source is it, and when was it created?  
8. For a given claim, who is the claimant, and is that different from the source’s writer?  
9. Which claims were made or endorsed by the credited author themselves?  
10. Which sources were copied or derived from other sources?  
11. Which sources are lost, and which still survive, and where?  
12. Which authorship claims come from letters, and who sent each letter, to whom, and when?  
13. Which claims come from statistical studies, and do they agree with the historical sources?  
14. In which publications did essay E appear, when, and under what number? Where did it first appear?  
15. Which essays first appeared in a book rather than a newspaper?  
16. What number does essay E have in the newspapers, and what number in the McLean edition?  
17. Which McLean essays were split from, or moved within, the newspaper series?  
18. Which editions revised essay E’s text, and who made or approved the revisions?  
19. Which edition first named the author of each essay?  
20. Which essays discuss topic T, and who is credited with them?
